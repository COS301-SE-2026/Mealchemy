04 Design Principles

Design Principles

These six principles define how Mealchemy should feel and behave across every screen.

1

Consistency

Every screen feels authored by the same hand. Design tokens and reusable components prevent one-off colour, spacing, and interaction decisions.

2

Simplicity

The user sees only what they need for the current task. The interface reduces decision fatigue instead of presenting every option at once.

3

Responsiveness

Every interaction communicates a result. Loading, pressed, saved, and error states are visible so the app never feels silent.

4

Accessibility

Text is readable from a countertop distance, touch targets support imprecise taps, and colour is never the only indicator of state.

5

Warmth, Modernity & Luxury

Warm surfaces, refined spacing, serif headings, rich food photography, and gold accents create a modern, luxurious kitchen experience.

6

Intelligence Made Visible

Match percentages, pantry indicators, and suggestion cards explain why the system recommends something.
