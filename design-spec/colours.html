01 Colour Palette

Defined brand colours and contrast examples.

Mealchemy uses deep maroon brand colours, warm cream surfaces, and gold as the main accent colour.

Primary Burgundy HEX #5C0018 RGB 92, 0, 24

Used for primary actions, brand blocks, and the start of the main gradient.

Primary Primary Dark HEX #570013 RGB 87, 0, 19

Used for dark headers, logo backgrounds, and high-contrast text pairings.

Primary Light Primary Light HEX #70172A RGB 112, 23, 42

Used for subtle maroon variation, hover states, and secondary brand surfaces.

Gradient End Rose Maroon HEX #A8003A RGB 168, 0, 58

Used only as the brighter end of the brand gradient for energy and depth.

Accent Gold HEX #D4AF37 RGB 212, 175, 55

Used for highlights, selected states, logo details, and premium emphasis.

Accent Muted Deep Gold HEX #755B00 RGB 117, 91, 0

Used where gold needs to feel quieter, such as subtle labels or muted accents.

Tertiary Muted Muted Rose HEX #7A5C5C RGB 122, 92, 92

Used for tertiary supporting details that should not compete with primary actions.

Background Background Light HEX #FEF9F1 RGB 254, 249, 241

Used as the default light-mode page background and large calm canvas.

Accent Light / Surface Light Warm Surface HEX #F8F3EB RGB 248, 243, 235

Used for cards, inputs, panels, and light surfaces sitting above the page.

Text Muted Muted Grey HEX #6B7280 RGB 107, 114, 128

Used for helper text, timestamps, captions, and non-critical metadata.

Warm Gradient End Warm Cream HEX #F8F0E3 RGB 248, 240, 227

Used as the second colour in the warm background gradient.

Background Dark Dark Background HEX #1D1C17 RGB 29, 28, 23

Used for dark mode page backgrounds and the footer surface.

Surface Dark Dark Surface HEX #1A1A1A RGB 26, 26, 26

Used for dark mode cards, inputs, and grouped content surfaces.

Surface White White HEX #FFFFFF RGB 255, 255, 255

Used when pure white is needed for text or clean high-contrast surfaces.
