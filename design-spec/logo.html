03 Logo & Iconography

Logo and Iconography

The Mealchemy logo combines a square monogram mark with a serif wordmark. The preferred variant is gold on brand dark.

Usage Rules

Clear space
M

Leave space equal to the M lettermark height on every side.

Minimum size
M
40 x 40px
MEALCHEMY
120px wide

Monogram minimum is 40x40px. Wordmark minimum width is 120px.

Approved colours

Use burgundy, gold, cream, and dark surfaces only.

Avoid
StretchRecolourShadow

Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, outline, or add drop shadows.

Iconography

Icon system

Use Flutter Material Icons with a simple outlined style. Icons support actions and states but should not replace visible labels.
